WHO WE ARE

A team built to learn, create & innovate

We're students exploring technology, design, and the power of collaboration.

Curious minds. Shared ambition.

Group 8 is made up of Information Technology students developing practical skills in programming, web development, digital design, communication, and project management.

Our goal is to create clean, useful, and user-friendly digital projects while continuously improving our technical abilities.
